No. 62: Two speeds with a variable differential

One belt from a drum can turn a shaft at two speeds. One of them comes through gears hidden inside a pulley. A second belt turns one of those gears, so it can make that speed slower or faster.

How it works

The belt runs from the drum down to one of three pulleys. The fork slides it across while the drum keeps turning. On the middle pulley the shaft turns at the same speed as the drum, because that pulley is fixed to it.

The right-hand pulley carries a small bevel gear on a pin, between two big bevel gears. One big gear is fixed to the shaft. The other, the third gear, is fixed to the end pulley, which a second belt turns from the top shaft. If the third gear stood still, the shaft would turn twice as fast as the right-hand pulley.

With an open belt the third gear turns the same way as the drum, and its speed is taken off that double speed. With a crossed belt it turns the other way, and its speed is added on. Put the belt on Right, then try Open and Crossed and compare the shaft's speed. Choose Closed to see the pulley as it looks from outside.

In Brown's words

For transmitting two speeds, one of which is a different and variable motion. This is very similar to the last, except in the third bevel-gear being attached to a fourth pulley, at the right of the other three, and driven by a band from a small pulley on shaft above. When left-hand belt is on the pulley carrying the middle bevel-gear, and pulley at the right turns in the same direction, the amount of rotation of the third bevel-gear must be deducted from the double speed which the shaft would have if this gear was at rest. If, on the contrary, the right-hand belt be crossed so as to turn the pulley in an opposite direction, that amount must be added.

This model is rebuilt from Brown's drawing and description. His dotted lines show the gears hidden inside the pulleys. Here part of the right-hand pulley's rim is cut away, below the belt, so you can see them. The sizes, the tooth counts, the frame and the fork were made up. Brown draws the belt on the loose pulley and the second belt open.
