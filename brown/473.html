No. 473: Water air-pump

A simple pump for sucking bad air out of a deep pit. A tub hangs upside down in a bigger tub of water, and two levers lift it and let it down. Two flaps that only open upward make the air go one way.

How it works

Watch the upper tub go up and down in the water. As it rises, the flap on the pipe opens and gas from the pit flows up into the tub. As it falls, that flap shuts, the flap on top opens, and the gas is pushed out into the open air.

The water acts as a seal. Air cannot get in under the rim of the upper tub, so the only ways in and out are the two flaps. Lifting the tub leaves more room inside, so the air in it thins out and the gas from the pit is pushed in to fill it. Lowering the tub squeezes that gas, and it can only escape through the top. Brown says this pump was used to clear a heavy, choking gas out of a deep shaft.

Try a short stroke, then a full one. Count how much gas each stroke draws out. Choose Closed to see the tubs as they look from outside.

In Brown's words

Air-pump of simple construction. Smaller tub inverted in larger one. The latter contains water to upper dotted line, and the pipe from shaft or space to be exhausted passes through it to a few inches above water, terminating with valve opening upward. Upper tub has short pipe and upwardly-opening valve at top, and is suspended by ropes from levers. When upper tub descends, great part of air within is expelled through upper valve, so that, when afterward raised, rarefaction within causes gas or air to ascend through the lower valve. This pump was successfully used for drawing off carbonic acid from a large and deep shaft.

This model is rebuilt from Brown's drawing. Both tubs are cut open down the middle so the inside shows. The frame is drawn with two top beams so the ropes can pass between them, and the board under the tub, the way the pipe leaves the tub and all the sizes were made up. The dots show the gas. The water inside the upper tub dips and rises a little with the pressure; this is made bigger so you can see it.
