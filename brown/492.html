No. 492: Boat-detaching hook

A boat hangs from ropes by a hook that will not come off while the boat is held up. One pull on a thin rope turns a lever, and the hook lets go at once. The boat drops into the water, free of its ropes.

How it works

The boat hangs from the hook on the pin. The pin is on the end of the tongue, and the tackle pulls it upwards. The tongue wants to swing up, but the top end of the lever stands in its way. So the pin cannot leave the hook.

Pull the thin rope and the lever turns on the middle of the standard. Its top end swings down and away from the pin. Nothing holds the tongue now. The pull of the tackle swings it up, and the pin slides out of the hook. The boat drops away from the ropes.

Try pulling only a little. The lever moves, but its top end is still in the way, so the boat stays on the hook. Then pull all the way and see how far the lever must turn before the boat is let go.

In Brown's words

Boat-detaching hook (Brown & Level's). The upright standard is secured to the boat, and the tongue hinged to its upper end enters an eye in the level which works on a fulcrum at the middle of the standard. A similar apparatus is applied at each end of the boat. The hooks of the tackles hook into the tongues, which are secure until it is desired to detach the boat, when a rope attached to the lower end of each lever is pulled in such a direction as to slip the eye at the upper end of the lever from off the tongue, which being then liberated slips out of the hook of the tackle and detaches the boat.

This model is rebuilt from Brown's drawing. Brown shows an eye in the lever that the tongue passes through. Here the eye is drawn as a round stop that stands in the way of a pin on the tongue, so the release can be seen. The tongue is made a little longer and its end lower than in the drawing so the pin can swing clear of the hook. The hook is shown as a wide open cup. The tackle ropes, the beam above them, the shape of the lever and the rope are made up. The boat is not drawn. It would be fixed to the bottom of the standard, so it drops with it.
