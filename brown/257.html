No. 257: Pulley with a round groove

A round belt runs in a rounded groove cut into the rim of this wheel. The groove holds the belt in place. It is used for thin cords that drive light machines.

How it works

The groove is curved like a half pipe. The round belt lies at the bottom of it and cannot slip out sideways.

The belt touches the wheel only along the groove. It is pulled tight, so it grips and the wheel turns with it. The belt moves at the speed of the groove.

The small wheel has a smaller groove, so it turns faster. Watch the red beads and compare the speeds in the readouts.

In Brown's words

A concave-grooved pulley for a round band.

This model is rebuilt from Brown's drawing. Brown shows only the wheel and its shaft. The belt, the small wheel, the posts and the base were made up, and so were the sizes.
