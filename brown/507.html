No. 507: Epicyclic train for very slow motion

Seven gears are arranged so that two almost equal motions cancel each other. What is left is tiny. The arm at the top must go round 25,000 times to turn the big gear at the side just once.

How it works

The arm whirls round and its pair of gears rolls round the two gears below it. Those two gears hardly seem to move. The big gear at the side meshes with both tubes, one above its centre and one below, so the tubes can only turn opposite ways at the same speed.

The two gears on the arm are joined, so they must turn together. The upper pair of gears has 61 and 49 teeth and the lower pair 51 and 41. Those two ratios are almost the same, but not quite. To satisfy both, the tubes must creep by one turn for every 2,500 turns of the arm. The big gear has ten times as many teeth as the small bevel gears, so it turns ten times slower still.

At real speed you will not see the big gear move. Choose 200 times faster. The arm becomes a blur, the two pegs on the gears drift opposite ways, and the red peg on the big gear just creeps.

In Brown's words

Another form of epicyclic train designed for producing a very slow motion. m is a fixed shaft upon which is loosely fitted a long sleeve, to the lower end of which is fixed a wheel, D, and to the upper end a wheel, E. Upon this long sleeve there is fitted a shorter one which carries at its extremities the wheels, A and H. A wheel, C, gears with both D and A, and a train-bearing arm, m, n, which revolves freely upon the shaft, m, p, carries upon a stud at n the united wheels, F and G. If A have 10 teeth, C 100, D 10, E 61, F, 49, G 41, and H 51, there will be 25,000 revolutions of the train-bearing arm, m, n, for one of the wheel, C.

This model is rebuilt from Brown's drawing, with the tooth numbers he gives. With those numbers the two pairs of gears would not be the same distance apart, so the lower pair has slightly bigger teeth. The base, the bracket, the collars and the pegs were made up. The fast setting only speeds up time; the gears are the same.
