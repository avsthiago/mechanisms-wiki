No. 23: Belt kept tight by a weighted pulley

A big pulley drives a lower pulley that can be moved up and down. One long belt joins them, bent round two small pulleys that hang from a weighted rope. The weight lifts the small pulleys just enough to keep the belt tight wherever the lower pulley is put.

How it works

The big pulley on the left pulls one long belt round. The belt goes over a small hanging pulley, down and under the lower pulley, and back up over a second small pulley. Then it returns to the big pulley. That is how the lower pulley is turned.

The two small pulleys hang in a frame from a second rope. That rope goes up over two guide pulleys and down to a weight. The weight always pulls the frame up, so the belt is always tight. Lower the bottom pulley and the belt pulls the frame down with it. The weight rises by the same amount.

Try low, then high. Watch the hanging pulleys follow the lower pulley while the weight goes the other way. Then look closely at the two small pulleys. The belt passes them in opposite directions, so they turn opposite ways.

In Brown's words

A contrivance for transmitting rotary motion to a movable pulley. The pulley at the bottom of the figure is the movable one; if this pulley were raised or depressed, the belt would be slackened or tightened accordingly. In order to keep a uniform tension on the belt, a pulley, A, carried in a frame sliding between guides (not shown), hangs from a rope passing over the two guide-pulleys, B, B, and is acted upon by the balance weight, C, in such manner as to produce the desired result.

This model is rebuilt from Brown's drawing and description. Brown draws one small pulley. The belt passes it twice, in opposite directions, so here it is two free pulleys side by side on one pin. The supports are made up: the beam and hangers over the guide pulleys, the posts behind the pulleys, the sliding bearing and the floor. Brown says the hanging frame slides between guides. They are left out, as in his drawing. He does not say how the lower pulley is raised or lowered.
