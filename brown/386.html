No. 386: Folding library ladder

A ladder whose rounds are hinged to its two side pieces. When folded, the rounds shut up inside the sides and the whole ladder becomes one round pole. That makes it easy to store.

How it works

The ladder starts open, with the rounds flat between the sides. Then the rounds tip over, one by one in step. The right side piece slides up and in, and the gap between the sides gets smaller until the sides meet.

All four rounds are the same length and are hinged the same distance apart on both sides. So they always stay parallel to each other. As they tip, the right side moves along a small circle: it comes in and goes up. When the rounds stand upright, they lie in the slots and the sides close into one pole.

Slide the folding bar from Open to Partly open to Closed. Watch the gap and the lean of the rounds in the readouts.

In Brown's words

Folding library ladder. It is shown open, partly open, and closed ; the rounds are pivoted to the side-pieces, which are fitted together to form a round pole when closed, the rounds shutting up inside.

This model is rebuilt from Brown's drawing. Brown shows the ladder open, half open and closed, so the model moves through all three. The number of rounds, their spacing and the shape of the slots were made up. The side pieces are drawn square instead of half round. The notch at the top of each side piece is left out.
