No. 458: Pulley and two buckets

A rope runs over a wheel with a bucket on each end. When one bucket goes down, the other comes up. It is a common way to raise water from a well.

How it works

The rope is one length. When one bucket goes down, the other has to come up by the same distance. The pulley just turns the rope round.

The full bucket is heavier than the empty one, so it wants to fall. To raise it, you pull down on the empty bucket. You only pull the difference between the two weights.

Try Half full, then Full. Watch how the Pull needed reading changes.

In Brown's words

The common pulley and buckets for raising water ; the empty bucket is pulled down to raise the full one.

This model is rebuilt from Brown's drawing. The hut, the shaft, the water in the bottom of the well and the weights are made up. A steady rhythm stands in for the person who pulls. The step on the post in Brown's picture is shown as a small shelf.
