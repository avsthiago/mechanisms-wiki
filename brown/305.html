No. 305: Single-pin escapement

Most escape wheels have many teeth. This one is a tiny disc with a single pin set off its centre. The disc makes half a turn for each swing of the pendulum, and the pin gives the pendulum a push on the way round.

How it works

The disc is always trying to turn anticlockwise. The pin is on the right of the disc and wants to rise, but it is stopped under the level face of the upper pallet. The pendulum swings on, and the level face just slides over the pin. The disc stands dead still.

When the pendulum swings to the left, the corner of the upper pallet slides off the pin. The pin is free to go over the top of its circle. On the way it presses on the upright face of the pallet and pushes the pendulum further to the left. Half a turn later the pin is coming down on the left, and it lands on the level face of the lower pallet. On the swing back, that pallet slides away, and the pin goes round underneath and pushes the pendulum to the right.

The pendulum sets the pace, and the disc makes half a turn for every beat. A long pendulum swings more slowly, so the beats come further apart. Try Short, then Long, and count the beats.

In Brown's words

A single-pin pendulum escapement. The escape-wheel is a very small disk with single eccentric pin ; it makes half a revolution for every beat of the pendulum, giving the impulse on the upright faces of the pallets, the horizontal faces of which are dead ones. This can also be adapted to watches.

This model is rebuilt from Brown's drawing and description. The shapes of the two pallets were worked out from his words, as plain blocks with one level and one upright face. The level faces are very slightly curved, as parts of circles round the top pin, so that the resting pin is not moved. The rod and bob under the plate, the back plate, the base and the sizes were made up. The two round pieces near the bottom of the plate are taken to be weights. The weight that turns the disc is left out and its pull is taken to be steady. The pin slides on the pallets without rubbing, and only the pendulum and the disc are slowed a little.
