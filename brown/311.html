No. 311: Double three-legged gravity escapement

A weight tries to turn two three-legged wheels on one shaft. Two hanging frames, called pallets, take turns to stop a leg. The wheels lift each pallet a little, and the pallet then pushes the pendulum with its own weight as it falls back. The wheels never touch the pendulum, so every push is the same.

How it works

Start with the pallet whose stop has a leg resting on it. The wheels cannot turn. A green pin near the middle holds this pallet pushed out a little way.

The pendulum swings out and meets the pin at the bottom of that pallet. It carries the pallet further out, and the stop slides off the leg. The wheels jump on. As they turn, another green pin pushes the other pallet out, and a leg of the other wheel lands on its stop. Then the pendulum swings back and the first pallet falls with it. Its lifting pin has moved away, so it falls further than it was carried. That extra fall is the push that keeps the pendulum swinging.

The push comes from the weight of the pallet, not from the wheels. Try Weak, then Strong. The wheels jump faster, but the pendulum swings just as far as before. A clock like this keeps good time even when the pull on its wheels changes.

In Brown's words

Double three-legged gravity escapement. Two locking-wheels, A, B, C, and a, b, c, are here used with one set of lifting-pins between them. The two wheels are set wide enough apart to allow the pallets to lie between them. The teeth of the first-mentioned locking-wheel are stopped by a stop-tooth, D, on one pallet, and those of the other one by a stop-tooth, E, on the other pallet.

This model is rebuilt from Brown's drawing. The lifting pins are larger and further from the middle than Brown shows, so you can see them work. The pendulum hangs in front, where Brown shows it behind. The fly is shorter than drawn and is fixed to the wheels. The spurs on the ends of the legs, the shape of the arms, the back plate, the rest pins, the bob, the base and all the sizes were made up. The weight that turns the wheels is left out. Nothing rubs in this model. Only the air slows the pendulum and the fly.
