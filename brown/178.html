No. 178: Variable speed for a slotting tool

A crank turns at a steady speed, but its sliding block is held by a round slot that sits off to one side of the shaft. As the crank turns, it gets longer and shorter. The tool slide moves slowly while it cuts and quickly on the way back, so little time is wasted.

How it works

The crank turns round at a steady speed. A red block rides in the crank slot. The block is also held in a round groove in the fixed plate. The groove is not centred on the shaft.

Near the bottom of the groove the block is close to the shaft, so the crank is short there and the rod moves slowly. This is the cutting stroke. On the far side the block is far from the shaft, so the crank is long and the rod moves fast. This is the return. The crank itself never changes speed.

Slide the offset to None and the block stays the same distance from the shaft. Then try Small and Large. Watch the Stroke reading and how the tool slide speeds up and slows down.

In Brown's words

Contrivance for varying the speed of the slide carrying the cutting tool in slotting and shaping machines, etc. The driving-shaft works through an opening in a fixed disk, in which is a circular slot. At the end of the said shaft is a slotted crank. A slide fits in the slot of the crank and in the circular slot; and to the outward extremity of this slide is attached the connecting-rod which works the slide carrying the cutting tool. When the driving-shaft rotates the crank is carried round, and the slide carrying the end of the connecting-rod is guided by the circular slot, which is placed eccentrically to the shaft; therefore, as the slide approaches the bottom, the length of the crank is shortened and the speed of the connecting-rod is diminished.

This model is rebuilt from Brown's drawing. Brown shows the slotted crank, the round groove and the start of a rod. The back plate, the rails, the tool slide, the cutting bit, the rod length and the base were made up. The sliding bar that carries the rod pin is drawn along the crank slot. The offset control was added for comparison.
