No. 147: Fan governor

A spinning arm carries two big flat fans. The faster it goes, the harder the air pushes back on them, and the more the arm lags and climbs. That lifts a lever that shuts down the steam, so the engine keeps a steady speed.

How it works

The engine turns the spindle. A round plate on the spindle has two sloping steps. The cross-head sits loose on the spindle, and two rollers under its arms rest on the slopes. So the slopes push the cross-head round. Two big flat fans are fixed to its arms.

Air pushes back on the fans, and the faster they go, the harder it pushes. At low speed the push is small, and the weight keeps the rollers at the bottom of the slopes. At higher speed the fans fall behind the spindle, and the rollers climb. That lifts the cross-head and the ball. The lever then pulls the rod up, which closes the steam valve a little. When the engine slows, the weight pulls it back down and the valve opens.

Move the speed slider from slow to fast and watch the lag and the lift. Then try a heavy cross-head. It needs a higher speed before it starts to rise.

In Brown's words

Steam engine governor. The operation is as follows:— On engine starting the spindle revolves and carries round the cross-head to which fans are attached, and on which are also fitted two friction-rollers which bear on two circular inclined planes attached securely to the center shaft, the cross-head being loose on the shaft. The cross-head is made heavy, or has a ball or other weight attached, and is driven by the circular inclined planes. As the speed of the center shaft increases, the resistance of the air to the wings tends to retard the rotation of the cross-head; the friction-rollers therefore run up the inclined planes and raise the cross-head, to the upper part of which is connected a lever operating upon the regulating-valve of the engine.

This model is rebuilt from Brown's drawing. Brown draws the rollers on curved inclines under the arms. Here the inclines are two sloping steps on a round plate on the spindle. The base, the long floor plate, the post and pivot of the lever, the hanging rod, the sizes and the strength of the air push were made up. The cross-head rises smoothly as the speed rises. The weight choices were added for comparison.
