# Code of conduct

mechanisms.wiki is made for students. The people who work on it should be as welcoming as the site.

## What we expect
- Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers and with people whose first language is not English.
- Criticise the work, not the person. Say what is wrong and, if you can, how to fix it.
- Assume good faith. Ask before you conclude.
- Accept that a maintainer may decline a change; explain your case once, then let it rest.

## What is not acceptable
- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory language or jokes about any group of people.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Sustained disruption of issues, pull requests or discussions.

## What happens
Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close issues and pull requests, and block people who break this
code. To report a problem, contact a maintainer privately through their GitHub profile. Reports are kept
confidential.

This code is adapted in spirit from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org).
